## English (primary)

- Name: **Who Is The Undercover**
- Subtitle: **Party word game for 3+ players**
- Promotional text (optional): *One word is different. Find the player who doesn't belong.*
- Keywords: `party game,word game,undercover,spy,social deduction,impostor,group game,friends`
- Description:

```
One of you has a different word. Will you spot the undercover?

Who Is The Undercover is a party word game for 3 or more players. Everyone in the room gets a secret word — but the undercover gets a similar, slightly different one. Talk it out, compare clues, and vote out the player who doesn't belong.

How to play:
1. Create a room and share the 6-character code.
2. Everyone taps "I'm ready" and the host starts the game.
3. Each player secretly sees their word.
4. Discuss out loud and vote out the likely undercover.
5. Reveal the role and start the next round.

Features:
• 100 word pairs in English and Chinese
• No accounts — just a nickname and a room code
• Instant multiplayer with a simple, native iOS design
• Perfect for parties, trips, and classrooms

A great icebreaker for groups of 3 to 12.
```
