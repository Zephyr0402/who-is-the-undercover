Privacy Policy — Who Is The Undercover

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Who Is The Undercover ("the App") is a multiplayer party word game operated by Shijun Shen ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App processes and why.

Information we collect

The App has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access.

When you play, the App processes:

  • Nickname — the display name you type before creating or joining a room.
  • Room code — the six-character code shared with the other players.
  • Game state — readiness, role, word, votes, and online status for players in your room.
  • Technical logs — the server may record IP address, timestamps, and error information for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information for marketing.

How we use it

The information above is used only to run the game: to show players in a room, to deal words and roles, to count votes, and to keep the service secure and working.

Retention

Rooms and their player records are deleted automatically — within 24 hours after a room stops being active, or within 1 hour if the room is empty. Server logs are kept for a short period for operational and security purposes and are then deleted.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We store game data on a server we operate. Anyone with the room code can view nicknames and shared room state, such as readiness, online status, and vote results. Your secret word is sent to your device during play and can be revealed to the room if you are voted out as the undercover player. We may disclose information if required by law.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A nickname is the only user-entered content; please do not use a real name.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS/WSS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we process.

Your choices

You can choose any nickname and can leave a room at any time. Because rooms are deleted automatically and we do not maintain accounts, there is normally no stored profile to delete. For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@bvshen.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL with an updated date.
